Weather, remembered.

Follow up to 10 cities and build a twice-daily weather history from two independent sources.

Your cities

Up to 10 cities. One calm list.

Search for a city and add it. Weather Insights keeps a separate history for each one, in its own local time.

Twice a day

Recorded at 12am and 12pm.

At midnight and midday local time, each city gets a reading from two independent services, Open-Meteo and MET Norway, stored side by side. It's a record of what happened, not a forecast.

History

See how it really was.

Browse daily readings in a table, or follow day and night temperatures on charts, from the last week to the last year. Show everything in °C or °F.

Digests and alerts, on your schedule.

Get a daily or weekly digest of your cities, and an alert when a reading goes outside, or inside, a range you set.
